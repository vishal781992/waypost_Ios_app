What the switch says, and does
Allowed
Days you are already busy on are marked on the trip
Not asked
Asks permission to read your calendar when you turn this on
Write only
ParkHop may add trips but not read them — turn on to allow reading too
Refused
Calendar access is off — tap to open Settings
iOS never asks twice, so once access is refused the row could only fail. It opens Settings instead. A control whose only outcome is failure is not a control.
