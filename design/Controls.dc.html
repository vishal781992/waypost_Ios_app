Measured against the app, not invented
Glass in a pill, pressed at 0.98 — the park screen’s “Plan a trip”, the leg sheet’s “Start Live Activity”. Forty-eight points where a control stands alone; fifty-two in a frozen footer, matching the share disc it stands beside, which is where My list already settled it.

In the stat line

A count, not a colour: a red outline says the trip is impossible and gives no way to act. The chip stays a state rather than a control, like the packs pill beside it — the day row that names the clash is what opens on a tap.
A clear trip shows nothing here. Silence is not a claim.
What the switch says, and does
Allowed
Days you are already busy on are marked on the trip
Not asked
Asks permission to read your calendar when you turn this on
Write only
ParkHop may add trips but not read them — turn on to allow reading too
Refused
Calendar access is off — tap to open Settings
iOS never asks twice, so once access is refused the row could only fail. It opens Settings instead. A control whose only outcome is failure is not a control.
