The road-trip answer: a duffel goes in a boot, not an overhead locker, which is the register this app actually lives in. Less immediate than a hard case, and the rounded body is closer in silhouette to the bookmark than the others are.
Worth weighing before picking: a waypost is a milepost. Trading it for a suitcase is a decision about what the app is called, not only about what is easiest to read at 29 points.
