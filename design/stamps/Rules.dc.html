How a stamp is decided
Standing in a park is not standing on its pin
Every coordinate the app holds — the sixty-two national parks, the three thousand state parks, the park service’s own units — is one point in the middle. A flat mile around that point is wrong at both ends of the scale.
The problem
A mile from the middle of Yellowstone is still Yellowstone
Yellowstone is sixty miles across. You can spend four days inside it and never come within thirty miles of the pin. Hovenweep is half a square mile — there, a mile is generous and right. One number cannot serve both.
Never offered. You are 24 miles from a pin in the middle.
Offered. The circle is the size of the park, plus your mile.
The rule
The reach is the park’s own size, plus your mile
Treat the park as a circle of the same area, and add a mile of grace on top — your number, kept as the thing it is good at: forgiving a GPS fix, a car park outside the gate, a boundary the data draws roughly. Never less than three miles all in, so the smallest monuments and a bad fix are both covered.

The dwell
Fifteen minutes inside, and only then
The notice comes the moment you cross. The automatic stamp waits out the fifteen minutes and checks you are still there — a drive-through on the highway that clips the circle leaves and never stamps. Leaving stops the clock; coming back starts it again.
Because it cannot be undone
Generous with the offer, strict with the automatic
Nothing in the app takes a stamp back, so the two thresholds are deliberately different. Offering is loose: if you might be there, it says so, and one tap is yours to make. Stamping for you is tight: inside the reach, fifteen unbroken minutes, and a fix good to a hundred metres or better. Anything less waits for your thumb.

What has to be built first
An area for each park. Nothing on the phone knows how big anything is. One column, added at build time from Wikidata, next to the coordinates already shipped.
A book that is not written down. The twelve stops are hard-coded in curated.json with no coordinates at all. That list is why the page cannot move.
Watching instead of asking once. Location is fetched once per screen today. Stamping needs regions the system watches for us — twenty at a time, the nearest twenty.
