All public API
Six layers, no private tricks
The Fitness awards use a Metal shader Apple does not ship to anyone. Everything below is available to this app, and stacks to the same effect.
1
The tilt itself
Device attitude, forty times a second, smoothed. On a Mac or in a preview it falls back to still.
CMMotionManager.deviceMotion → roll, pitch
2
The turn
Real perspective, not a skew. Anchored behind the card so it pivots like an object on a table.
rotation3DEffect(_:axis:anchor:anchorZ:perspective:)
3
The specular
A soft highlight whose centre is the tilt. This is what sells metal.
RadialGradient with a moving UnitPoint
4
The foil
An angular gradient rotated by the tilt, screened over the engraving at low opacity.
AngularGradient · .blendMode(.colorDodge)
5
Depth
The engraving and the type move a point or two against the ground, so the card has thickness.
offset(x: roll * 2) on the inner layers
6
The good version
Where you want true iridescence rather than a rotated gradient: a fragment shader, from iOS 17.
ShaderLibrary · .colorEffect(_:isEnabled:)
Reduce Motion has to switch off 1, 2 and 5 and leave the card still and lit from one side. A pass that lurches is a pass somebody turns off.
